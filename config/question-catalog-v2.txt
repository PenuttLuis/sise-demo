INTENT 1: Explain municipality prioritization
Triggers:
- "Why is this municipality prioritized?"
- "Explain this municipality"
- "Why is it ranked here?"
- Click on municipality

Response:
- Return the full structured explanation.
- Mention the top 2 drivers when they are clearly different from the others.
- If there is a tie at the top, use the neutral combined summary.

INTENT 2: What does a factor mean?
Triggers:
- "What is Riesgos?"
- "Explain Pobreza"
- "What does Índice SAN mean?"
- "What are Políticas?"

Response:
- Return only the fixed definition of the factor.

Examples:
Riesgos:
"Riesgos refleja la vulnerabilidad territorial del municipio frente a amenazas y limitaciones de respuesta."

Pobreza:
"Pobreza refleja carencias estructurales asociadas a pobreza y necesidades básicas insatisfechas."

Índice SAN:
"El Índice SAN refleja condiciones relacionadas con la seguridad alimentaria y nutricional."

Políticas:
"Políticas refleja la presencia del municipio en programas, estrategias o instrumentos de priorización pública, no una condición de vulnerabilidad."

INTENT 3: What is the ranking based on?
Triggers:
- "How is the ranking calculated?"
- "What is ponderación?"
- "Why this score?"

Response:
"La ponderación total es la suma de cuatro componentes: Riesgos, Pobreza, Índice SAN y Políticas. Cada uno aporta hasta 25 puntos, para un máximo de 100. El ranking se deriva de esta ponderación total."

INTENT 4: General help
Triggers:
- "What is this?"
- "How does this work?"
- No municipality selected

Response:
"Este sistema muestra la priorización municipal basada en factores de vulnerabilidad (Riesgos, Pobreza, Índice SAN) y referencias institucionales (Políticas). Seleccione un municipio o consulte sobre un factor para más detalle."

INTENT 5: Follow-up questions
Triggers:
- "Why is Riesgos high?"
- "Which factor is strongest?"
- "What are the main drivers?"
- "Why are Políticas high?"

Response:
- Reuse the selected municipality context.
- Answer briefly.
- Mention the top 1 or top 2 drivers.
- Do not regenerate the full explanation unless explicitly requested.

INTENT 6: Driver comparison
Triggers:
- "What are the top two factors?"
- "Which factors explain the ranking the most?"
- "What weighs most here?"

Response:
- Identify the top 2 factor scores.
- Explain them using the approved wording.
- If one of them is Políticas, explicitly clarify that it reflects institutional presence, not vulnerability.

DISPLAY RULES
- Always show factors in this order:
  1. Riesgos
  2. Pobreza
  3. Índice SAN
  4. Políticas
- Always classify each factor as Bajo, Medio, or Alto.
- The UI may color the level label:
  - Bajo = green
  - Medio = yellow
  - Alto = red
- Color is supportive only; the meaning must still be explicit in text.
